# Astartes Forge v3.0.15 — Space Wolves A4 Artwork

## Summary
The final approved Space Wolves A4 portrait border is now integrated as the app's active A4 Rand Artwork.

## Artwork integration
- One uninterrupted open title plaque at the top.
- Fenrisian runes, wolf pelts, ice, icicles, chains, fangs and wolf ornamentation retained.
- Side ornaments extend inward and are allowed to sit beneath live text panels.
- Lower border is deliberately calmer so overlapping text boxes remain visually acceptable.
- The central content opening is transparent; the physical frame remains layered behind live content.

## Renderer
- v3.0.14 A4 full-bleed geometry is unchanged: 210 × 297 mm physical page with 226 × 317 mm artwork overscan.
- The A4 Space Wolves live header is aligned with the artwork's title plaque.
- A5 remains unchanged.

## Unchanged
ROSZ import, validation/rules libraries, datasheet logic and print-button flow.
